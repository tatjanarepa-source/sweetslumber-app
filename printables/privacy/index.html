Privacy for the free printables

Last updated October 2026

This page explains what happens to your email address when you sign up for the free printable drawing pages from Toddler Activity Book Ages 2–4 by Sophia’s Tiny Hands.

What I collect

  • Your email address.
  • The date and time you signed up and ticked the consent box.
  • Whether you open or click my emails, which my email service records automatically.

I don’t ask for your name, and I don’t collect anything about your child.

Why I use it

  • To send you the printable drawing pages.
  • To send one short follow-up asking how your little one enjoyed the book.
  • To let you know now and then about new books.

I only email you because you agreed to it when signing up. I never sell or share your email address.

Where it’s stored

Your email address is stored by MailerLite, the email service I use to send these emails. They keep it secure and only use it to send emails on my behalf.

How long I keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing books@sweetslumber.app. I’ll take care of it promptly.

If you’re unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK, or to the data protection authority in your country.
